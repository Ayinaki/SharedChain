# AyinChallenge: Future Feature Ideas

This document tracks potential improvements and new features to enhance the "Chained Together" and "Shared Health" experience.

## 1. Implemented Features (Completed)
*   **Regen Sponsor Indicator**: A green heart (`❤`) next to the current "Support" player's name. (Lingers for 2s).
*   **Wipe Dramatics**: Configurable sound effect played on team wipe (Default: Illusioner Blindness).
*   **Post-Run Summary**: Displays "Support" and "Victim" awards after a wipe.

## 2. New Team Synergy Ideas
*   **Rescue Anchor**: 
    *   *Concept*: Sneaking allows players to "plant their feet," making them much harder to be pulled. Allows the team to save someone falling into a pit.
*   **Shared XP**:
    *   *Concept*: All experience orbs collected are distributed evenly among the linked team members.
*   **The Huddle**:
    *   *Concept*: If the entire team crouches close together, the shared regeneration speed is significantly increased.
*   **Health Color Dynamics**:
    *   *Concept*: Player names and icons change color (Green -> Yellow -> Red) based on the shared health pool's percentage.

## 3. Exploration & Loot Ideas
*   **Link Compass**:
    *   *Concept*: A compass that points toward the furthest teammate, helping with navigation in complex terrain like caves or forests.
*   **Loot Hype**:
    *   *Concept*: Finding rare items (Diamonds, etc.) gives the whole team a temporary "Luck" or "Speed" boost to celebrate the find.

## 4. Technical Ideas (Restored)
*   **Smart Portal Redirection**:
    *   *Concept*: Ensure that if one player enters a portal, the entire chain is pulled through or warned immediately to prevent chain-snapping across dimensions.
